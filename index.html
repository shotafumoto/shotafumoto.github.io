We Are Nobody

These are the most valuable photographs I've ever taken.

With a soft, clouded mind, I wandered without any plan—
not knowing where I'd stay, which town I'd end up in,
or even what country I'd reach.
Each decision came from a quiet intuition in the moment.

And in that way of living—
the one I believe is most true to myself—
I encountered people like miracles.

These photographs hold all of my thoughts,
ideals, and quiet acts of courage.

I hope I can live the rest of my life
feeling those miracles again and again.
And I hope, through these photographs,
that I might share just a small part of what I believe in.

Self-published, 2025  ·  30 copies

About

Shota Fumoto is a Japanese photographer
based in Tbilisi, Georgia.

He photographs people as they are —
not the role they play, but the person underneath.
His work moves between documentary portraiture
and commercial photography, always looking
for the moment before the mask.
