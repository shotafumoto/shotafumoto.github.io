Bunkyo University Affiliated School.

A recruitment campaign for Bunkyo University Affiliated Junior & Senior High School, shot over thirteen days. Student portraits in golden hour against the Tokyo skyline, paired with architectural documentation of the campus — cherry blossoms behind glass curtain walls, curving staircases caught in late afternoon light. Direction, photography, and post.
